### English (en-US)

**Title:** BrawlZone Bubble: Draft Helper

**Short description:** Tier list, best picks and counters floating over Brawl Stars, mid-draft.

**Full description:**

BrawlZone Bubble puts the Ranked meta on top of your game. A small bubble floats over Brawl Stars; tap it in the middle of a draft and the panel opens with the answer for the map you are on. Tap again and it folds away. You never leave the game.

WHAT IT ANSWERS, MID-DRAFT
• Best picks, map by map: the live Ranked rotation, ranked by what actually wins on that map, not a season-long average.
• Who to ban: the bans Ranked players make on this map, in order.
• Best trio: the three brawlers that win together on the map.
• Counters: enter the bans and the enemy picks, and it names what beats them.
• The build to run: gadget, star power and gears for any brawler you tap.
• By rank: Diamond and below, Mythic, Legendary or Masters and up, because the meta moves with rank.

MADE FOR YOUR ACCOUNT
• Add your player tag (optional) and the bubble ticks the brawlers you own, wears your player icon, and can hide everything you cannot play.
• Filter to Power 11 only, or Power 11 with hypercharge.
• See what to upgrade next with your coins and power points.
• Save several accounts and switch with one tap.

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
• Drag the bubble anywhere; it tucks into the edge and fades while you play. Three sizes.
• Quick Settings tile: swipe down inside the game to show or hide the bubble without opening the app.
• Opens instantly and keeps the last list when you are offline.
• 22 languages.

HONEST NUMBERS
The stats come from BrawlZone (brawlzone.net), which samples real Ranked battles and refreshes every two hours. Every number shows how many battles are behind it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
• It does not read your screen, your match or your game account, and it does not touch the game.
• No ads, no sign-up, no in-app purchases.
• The only special permission is "Display over other apps", which the bubble needs to float.

BrawlZone is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Supercell. Brawl Stars is a trademark of Supercell Oy.

### Data safety (Play Console → App content → Data safety) — corrected

The older answer further down ("Collects data: No") is **wrong** for any
build that offers the player tag: `/api/bubble/roster` passes the tag to
`recordLookup`, which stores it with the player's public game stats in
`sampled_players` for battle sampling.

- Does the app collect or share user data? **Yes, collects** (shares: No).
- Data type: **App info and performance? No. Personal info → User IDs: Yes**
  (the in-game player tag).
  - Collected, not shared. **Optional** (the app works without a tag).
  - Processed ephemerally? **No** (stored).
  - Purposes: **App functionality** (owned brawlers, upgrade plan) and
    **Analytics** (the tag joins the pool whose public battles build the stats).
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes** (HTTPS to brawlzone.net only).
- Can users request deletion? **Yes**: by email, as /privacy says (the tag's
  saved stats are deleted and it is excluded from future sampling).
- Privacy policy URL: https://brawlzone.net/privacy

## Title

BrawlZone Bubble — Brawl Stars tier list overlay

## Short description (80 chars)

A floating tier list over Brawl Stars. Overlay, Quick Settings tile, no ads.

## Full description

BrawlZone Bubble is a floating overlay for Brawl Stars. It draws a small movable bubble on top of the game — something no website or browser tab can do — and a tap opens the current Ranked tier list right there, mid-draft, on the mode and the map you are playing. Tap again and it folds away.

What is native
• The bubble itself: a system overlay ("Display over other apps") that floats above Brawl Stars and any other app, drags anywhere, snaps to the nearest edge, and closes by dropping it on the ✕.
• A Quick Settings tile: swipe down from inside the game and tap BrawlZone to put the bubble up or take it down, without opening the app. On Android 13 and up the app adds the tile for you with one tap.
• A foreground service with a persistent notification while the overlay is showing, with Panel and Stop buttons in the shade.
• In-app updates: the app checks brawlzone.net for a newer version and downloads and installs it itself.

What the panel shows
• The full Ranked tier list, S through D, scored on adjusted win rate and pick rate from sampled competitive battles, refreshed every two hours.
• Down to the map: Ranked hands you one map from its pool and the answer moves with it. Pick the mode, then the map; both are remembered.
• A draft board: enter the bans and the picks on both sides and it ranks the brawlers left for you.
• Builds on tap: the star power, gadget and gears each brawler's owners actually run, with the sample size behind every number.

The panel's content is served live from brawlzone.net, so the numbers are the same as the site's and update without an app update. Everything around it — the overlay, the tile, the service, the notification, the updater — is the app.

What it does not do
• It does not read your screen, your match, or your account. It needs "Display over other apps", which Android asks you to grant in Settings, and nothing else.
• No ads, no account, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
• It does not touch the game. It shows the meta; you match it to the draft in front of you.

BrawlZone is a fan-made statistics site and is not affiliated with Supercell. Brawl Stars is a trademark of Supercell Oy.

## Review notes (Amazon; Uptodown has no field for this)

The app draws a small overlay over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW), which is its entire purpose, and adds a Quick Settings tile (TileService) that toggles it from the shade. The panel inside the overlay is a WebView of brawlzone.net/bubble/panel; the overlay, tile, service and updater are native: a tier list you can consult while a game is in the foreground. It runs a foreground service with a persistent notification while the overlay is showing, and stops when the user closes the bubble. Beyond that it asks only for network state (a normal install-time permission), so that with no connection the panel shows the last saved list instead of an error; it collects no data. To test: open the app, tap Start, allow "Display over other apps" when sent to Settings, return; a draggable bubble appears; tap it to open the panel.

## Data safety answers (OUTDATED — see the corrected Google Play section at the top)

Collects data: No. Shares data: No. Encrypted in transit: Yes (HTTPS to brawlzone.net only). Data deletion: nothing is stored server-side. The app makes network requests to brawlzone.net to load the panel and check for updates; no identifiers are sent.
